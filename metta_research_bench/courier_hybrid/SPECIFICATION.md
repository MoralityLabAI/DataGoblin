# Native courier action equivalence and bounded verification

Fix a world (W=(G,O,P,d,e,F_0,F_1)). The directed arcs of (G) are open or permit-gated. (O), (P), (d), and (e) are the issuing offices, pickup sites, desk, and entry. (F_i) is the set of outlets for evidence identity (i). The two identities are independent by construction. All outlets in (F_i) attest the same source.

The immutable physical state is (s=(x,p,c,E)), where location (x) is a site, (p) is a Boolean permit, cargo (c) is waiting, carrying, or delivered, and (E\subseteq\{0,1\}) is the attested identity set. The initial state is ((e,0,\mathrm{waiting},\varnothing)). A typed move follows one admissible arc. Issue is legal at (O) with no permit. Collect is legal at (P) with waiting cargo. Attest (i) inserts (i) at (F_i) if it is absent. Deliver requires carrying cargo, (x=d), and (E=\{0,1\}). Delivered states have no primitive successors. The endpoint is delivery followed by an accepted STOP. A permit is required only when a route uses a gated arc. STOP costs one verifier call and zero primitive actions.

## Objectives

Let (A(s)) be the legal primitive actions and (T(s,a)) their deterministic transition. For the primitive certificate schedule, let (Q(s,h,b;a)) be the best lexicographic value ((\text{verified completion},-\text{primitive length},-\text{call count})) with first action (a), at most (h) actions and (b) verifier calls. An accepted STOP contributes ((1,0,-1)). Each legal primitive with (h>0,b>0) evaluates the successor's maximum value (V(T(s,a),h-1,b-1)) and debits one length and one call. Use a failure sentinel for unreachable completion. Evaluate the maximum over feasible actions including STOP, and retain training states with value starting in 1.

The exact shortest action set is (A^*(s,h,b)=\arg\max_a Q(s,h,b;a)) on those states. The current primitive tariff is uniform, so all shortest successes have the same call count. The budget-aware action set (A^+(s,h,b)) includes every first action admitting full verified continuation under both caps. It can contain longer alternatives. A reverse shortest-path traversal over independently cross-checked native successors computes both sets. It omits unreachable successors, and compares one plus successor distance with (h) and two plus that distance with (b). The extra call verifies STOP.

Use one matched training row per visited state per root across two extreme shortest demonstrations. Use the smallest action ID for canonical supervision and SHA-256 of the typed state and world identity, reduced modulo (|A^*|), for the hashed control. The joint loss is

\[
\mathcal L=-\log\sum_{a \in A_\mathrm{input}(s)}w_a p_\theta(a\mid s,h,b).
\]

Use weight 1 on the designated singleton, all of (A^*), or all of (A^+), respectively. Soft supervision assigns each feasible first action weight (2^{-(m_a+k_a-m_*-k_*)/2}), where (m) and (k) are primitive moves and verification calls on its cheapest certified continuation. Infeasible actions have weight 0. The maximum weight is 1. A two-unit increase in combined cost halves the weight. Only public facts, current obligations and counters, and the local allowed-action mask enter the pointer. Future continuation values enter training weights and the explicit reference only.

## Transactions and reachable-state equivalence

The pinned MeTTa relation authorizes each physical transition. Evidence rules authorize attestations and guard delivery. A macro is a typed tuple of primitives whose length fits the remaining horizon. A single native query recursively derives its whole proof, including the ordered intermediate states and final state. The session publishes that final state only after exact agreement with the independent projection. The receipt binds the rules and world facts, query, actions, initial and final states, and each proof step. A rejection spends its query, emits no accepted-step digests, and leaves the physical state and committed route unchanged. Macro depth bounds native proof work as well as the candidate search; a query on a length-four macro performs more work than a one-step query.

For fixed (W), tariff, and bounded macro vocabulary, call states equivalent at ((h,b)) when the languages of successful certified suffixes (action tokens with their resource charges) are equal. This equivalence preserves optimal success and cost, by taking the minimum realized cost over the same suffix language. The recursion follows by partitioning nonempty suffixes by the first certificate and reducing the budgets by its length and tariff. Equality of optimal value alone is coarser: two states may have the same optimum via different legal continuations. Likewise two first actions with the same eventual success can have distinct tariffs.

Every certified macro path has a primitive expansion by the MeTTa replay recurrence. With unlimited calls this leaves the physical reachable set unchanged. With a one-query macro tariff and a finite call allowance, it weakly expands reachable states relative to primitive-only certification whenever both can choose primitives. It can strictly expand the set: two legal consecutive transitions and STOP need three primitive-schedule queries, while the corresponding certified macro and STOP need two. This is a statement about available certificates, not equal engine work or a guarantee that a given proposer finds the macro.

Reserve repair acts only on the first unrepaired incomplete STOP and retains a call for terminal verification. Under the same deterministic policy and caps, its pretrigger prefix is identical to standard inference. Any standard success therefore survives, since it never triggers repair. Its paired gain is the frequency of rescued standard failures in this design. Early spending can alter the prefix and has no such guarantee. The learned gate can change which incomplete STOPs are repaired. For variants that can affect successes, the paired effect equals the rescue rate minus the regression rate.
