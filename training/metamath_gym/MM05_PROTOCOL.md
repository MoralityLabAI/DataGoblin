# MM05 scheduling control registration

Frozen before execution. Use MM04 public corpus as spent replay and the new MathCan MM05 artifact (source ordinals 601–2400, exclude prior alpha-signature families) as a fresh public evaluation. No earlier challenge root contributes training examples. Family key groups exact typed alpha-equivalent goal/hypothesis/DV signatures, not all semantic relatives or shared dependencies.

Policies: serial without early stop, round robin without early stop, serial stop at first exact certificate, round robin stop at first exact certificate, and both stop policies with exact transition cache keyed by (suffix position, entire token stack) scoped to a single theorem root. Train-only ridge allocator: use all legal candidate trace states from train roots, features [intercept, target equality, token multiset similarity, normalized stack length, normalized top length, remaining suffix length, progress fraction, prior rank fraction]. Fit regularized least squares to eventual validity and log1p(remaining actual transitions until termination), lambda=1, per-root total sample weight 1. At each step select maximal clipped predicted success probability divided by exp(clipped predicted log remaining cost), ties by symbolic order. Train from fresh train rows only for fresh evaluation; no hyperparameter selection. Charge exactly one primitive transition per cache miss or uncached evaluation, including failed steps; count cache hits separately, no cache across theorem roots. Stop only on a completed exact certificate, or exhaustion of budget. When unresolved, report choice correctness separately from certificate rate. Budgets 0/1/2/4/8/16/32/128, compare completed certificates and mean primitive cost. Oracle used for audit, train targets and retrospective scoring only. Report spent and fresh separately. This is a deterministic scheduling/linear allocator pilot; no BPD or neural intervention.

The scheduler must match all immediate and full candidate validity outcomes against the reference oracle before evaluation. Every cache key includes full immutable stack and position, and uses the identical suffix within each root. Cache hits do not equal external verifier calls. No wall-clock superiority follows from primitive counts. Source registration precedes runs; public family split is not sealed.
