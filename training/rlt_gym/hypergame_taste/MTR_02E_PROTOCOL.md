# MTR-02E: discovery versus certification, hard logical-work budgets

Registered public development experiment; successor to MTR-02D. Preserve all prior receipts.

## Worlds and ground truth
Use 128 Boolean witnesses (four nonlinear selector coordinates, three payload coordinates), positive integer priors, evidence-gated single- and paired-selector queries, branch-specific full and partial payload probes, and global nonlinear sketches. Cost and encoding families vary; held-out cubic construction and changed probe costs define a combined OOD shift. A probe is legal when its prefix gate is implied by the compatible set, its observation is informative, and its cost fits the remaining research credits. The prior, probe masks and terminal claim predicates are public. Deterministic informative probes never repeat, so the canonical state is (compatible mask, remaining credits). Full, partial, branch and coarse claims have distinct rewards/penalties; abstention is legal. Exhaustive integer Bellman oracle and independent weighted per-witness policy replay. Register 48 training roots and 24 ordinary / 24 OOD evaluation roots at default scale, with distinct deterministic public namespaces and content signatures. No private holdout.

## Work accounting and controls
Hard root-level logical-work budgets 8,192, 32,768 and 131,072 units; primary budget 32,768, comparisons at the other budgets descriptive. One unit per declared primitive loop iteration/lookup/partition cell/feature coordinate/backup component; count terminal and prior scans, cold cache work, legal-action scans, analytic-plan computations, scoring and propagation, and graph bookkeeping. Fixed-width 128-bit operations count as one logical operation where specified. This is an implementation-specific proxy, not CPU cycles, wall-clock or process RSS. Root generation and immutable truth/probe tables are shared evaluator inputs. Separate cold per-arm cache, no evaluator/oracle cache in the policy. If a work cap interrupts an operation, charge attempted work, leave the last complete feasible incumbent intact. A shared base policy chooses the best of terminal, one-probe-and-stop, and two deterministic constructive strategies. Strong analytic comparator: depth-limited contingent replanner (up to two additional exact action levels over that base), evaluated by constructing the contingent policy for all reachable outcomes offline under the same root-level work cap; retain the previous feasible plan if incomplete. Also report the uncapped online replan comparator's per-realized-path work (if executed); distinguish from the hard offline budget. No cross-arm warmed caches.

Anytime search keeps exact feasible lower bounds and optimistic upper bounds. Expansion introduces all legal actions and their outcome children; propagate bounds monotonically through the canonical DAG. Replay the frozen incumbent over all hidden witnesses. Every reported root bound must contain the oracle and every replay must equal its lower bound. Select optimistic frontier states by deterministic mass gap, work-aware mass gap, random (three independent seeds per root), and two learned priority heads. All use the same exact expansion/backup engine, terminal library and budget meter.

## Learned targets and decision
Train only on training roots (four state presentations per root maximum) by observing actual one-expansion change in root feasible value and root certificate gap per measured incremental logical work; no evaluator-only state enters the priority features. Deterministic regularized regression, same input features for discovery and certification. Report fit diagnostics, root paired utility, certified gap, closure, actual metered work and policy safety, plus state counts and control differences. Compare each head against strongest deterministic and random controls and strong analytic replanning at equal hard budgets. Public descriptive intervals only; no promotion unless evidence supports benefit over strong controls for the corresponding objective in both splits. Distinguish reaching oracle utility from closing a certificate gap. No claims of recurrence-specific or BPD activation benefit. Full native integration, gradient audit (if relevant), independent private campaign, CPU/RSS instrumentation and full CI remain separate.
