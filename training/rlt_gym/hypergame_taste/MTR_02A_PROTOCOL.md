# MTR-02A: costly mathematical evidence, learned recurrence

Registered public-development specification, prior to pilot execution. This is a follow-up to MATH_TASTE_V2.md; it is not the private confirmatory MTR-02 experiment.

## World and oracle
- Enumerate 16 hidden mathematical certificates and two nuisance states, with strictly positive integer prior masses. Full witnesses solve either a box LP, an invertible GF(2) system, or (held-out family) a triangular Boolean system. A weaker parity consequence may also be certified.
- Randomized, costed, finite observations include exact linear sketches, correlated noisy sketches and gated representation choices. Each probe can be used once. A setup action buys access to a representation and does not reveal hidden evidence. Depth K is a public announcement chain; vary K=0,4,8,12,16 independently of credit budget (6–8).
- Terminal macro-action picks the best publicly calculable certificate or abstention at the current posterior. Full correctness reward +16/-24, weaker consequence +4/-10. Costs subtract from utility. No policy observes the actual hidden world, oracle lookahead or hidden certificate ID. All arms can calculate the public posterior, immediate terminal values, and one-step features.
- Exact weighted Bellman recursion in integer arithmetic; verify by explicit replay over every latent world conditional on the announcement. Full certificate validity checked against original mathematical definitions. Include two-ply, myopic, fixed greedy information-per-credit and a public costed linear-sketch rank heuristic where applicable. Matched policies share action availability, cost, and no-repeat rules.
- Split by generation namespace (public deterministic training, ordinary development, OOD family). No claim of private holdout. Freeze 4 presentations per training root, mix oracle and exploratory histories. Report exact root counts and content overlap audit. OOD triangular Boolean systems have no training roots. Cost and nuisance patterns also shift; report these jointly rather than claiming isolated transfer.

## Learned arms
Use a small action-value scorer with common publicly derived action features and state-token history. Train by supervised regression to oracle Q values on the training split only. RLT learns a tanh state update with recurrent matrix. Matched damping applies the same learned matrices to fixed exponentially averaged *raw* previous state tokens; pooled stateless arm uses their arithmetic mean. Equal parameter counts, initialization seeds, presentations and update schedule. All have access to the same history and current-state sufficient statistics. No test-time weight updates. Use three model initialization seeds; average seed outcomes within root before paired intervals. Distinguish computation from verifier credit counts. Report terminal validity and utility, not just imitation accuracy.

## Decision
Public pilot primary diagnostic: positive mean utility gap over both learned controls, paired by root, with no increase of false-publication probability >0.005 and no >5% increase in actual query count (use paired descriptive intervals; no confirmatory claim). Compare against two-ply and the exact oracle. Treat ties/failures as results; no threshold tuning on development. A future private confirmatory experiment must freeze a power analysis, exact verifier compute and RAM budgets, separate test seeds and independent oracle. Full RLT training, BPD cache and native GPTStoryworld integration are separate follow-ups.
