# MTR-02H: paid proposal acquisition, mass-only caching and option returns

Registered public development successor to MTR-02G. Preserve previous receipts and negative results.

## Controlled worlds
Reuse the 128-witness nonlinear tree/cross-cut construction mechanism with fresh MTR-02H namespaces (32 training, eight calibration, 24 ordinary, 24 cubic/cost/cross-cut OOD roots). Exact integer Bellman oracle, full-scan versus indexed legality and explicit per-witness policy replay. Independent dense terminal aggregation in oracle. Root generation, oracle and replay remain evaluator-only.

## Cache and bound interventions
Separate weighted mass/cardinality lookup from terminal claim-bin aggregation. A mass-only query scans each set bit once, caches mass/count, and never builds claim bins. A full statistics computation reuses a cached mass/count and computes the four claim-bin categories; if mass is not cached, compute both in the same scan. Charge cache lookups, insertions, witness visits and bin updates. Compare eager all-stream portfolios with identical policy and full-stats versus mass-only information lookup; use fresh caches and matched work caps. Verify cached mass/count against independent direct enumeration at sampled oracle states; verify both implementations return identical information proposals with uncapped work on those states.

Additional admissible bound: let m_min be the minimum prior weight of any world and c_min the cheapest public probe. For a nonempty child C with mass W and cardinality n, an immediate full claim earns at most 36W-90m_min when n>1, while every partial or weaker claim earns at most 24W. If n=1, its upper bound is 36W. Otherwise the maximum of these terminal bounds and (36-c_min)W (only if at least c_min credits remain) is admissible. Apply this after each legal first action and maximize with exact immediate terminal value. Intersect with the existing clairvoyant bound. Audit against exact V* and the free-observation relaxation from G on 64 sampled visited states per root for the first four ordinary and four OOD evaluation roots. Measure cold charged work and strict tightening of each bound separately. Use the cheaper bound as a paid certification option, not as a free oracle input.

## Paid stream acquisition and multi-job credit
At an option boundary choose one of route, information, immediate, optimistic, or the new bound-refinement stream, using public root features and previous completed option summaries only. Acquire only that stream's next proposal; its frontier traversal, lookup and scoring are charged. If a chosen action stream has no preferred job, try the optimistic action stream (also charged). Execute up to four jobs per option, with transactional backups and retained incumbent. An option may be interrupted by its quantum; keep all completed jobs and record charged work, lower gain, gap reduction, completed jobs and censoring. No candidate stream is acquired for free merely to rank it. Compare single route, eager all-stream mass-gap portfolio with full or split cache, deterministic route-first portfolio (switch after two route options with no lower gain), equal-length round-robin and three seeded random option portfolios, learned gain/work and learned gap/work portfolios. All lazy option policies have the same available stream IDs and fallback. Retain the best root lower plan and monotone bounds, including at interruption.

Train two deterministic regularized value models and one censored log-cost model from up to four option presentations per root; mix public option IDs and fixed quanta 2048,8192,32768,65536. Targets are actual committed multi-job returns, including acquisition overhead and partial progress, never oracle future values. Features are public root statistics, remaining/assigned quantum, option ID, previous completed per-stream summary and decision count. At evaluation score all stream IDs without acquiring their proposals. Charge feature/scoring work and acquisition cost. Report censoring and cost model limitations; no claim that selected samples are counterfactual causal targets.

## Calibration and decision
Choose the smallest budget from 8192/16384/32768/65536/131072/262144 for which both route and deterministic route-first portfolio complete at least eight action jobs on the median calibration root, and at least six of eight roots complete at least two option decisions in each. If none qualifies report resource failure and use largest descriptively. Evaluate primary and half-primary on fresh roots. Hard logical work caps, complete policy replays, bound checks, operation and logical storage counters, root-paired descriptive intervals. No promotion unless learned gains or certification improve on strong controls in both splits; report utility cost of certification. Cache benchmark and bound audit are separate from policy improvement. A native microbenchmark of the mass-only primitive (if feasible) is a separate measured CPU/RSS diagnostic, not end-to-end native integration. Public development only; no native GPTStoryworld/TRL/BPD activation benefit, full new CI, private holdout or 1B training claim.
