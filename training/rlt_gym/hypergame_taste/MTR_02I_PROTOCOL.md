# MTR-02I: paired option intervention and route-anchored policy

Registered public development experiment, successor to MTR-02H. Keep preceding pilot receipts intact. The new experiment tests whether counterfactual credit assignment and a conservative route anchor improve scheduling.

## Worlds and policy
Use the same 128-world nonlinear finite research mechanism, with fresh namespaces for training, calibration, ordinary evaluation and family/cost/cross-gate OOD. Independent dense terminal oracle, indexed versus full legality and weighted replay. Pure bound stream may execute only bound jobs, never an action fallback; other streams may use the existing optimistic action fallback. If pure bound is exhausted, a subsequent decision can choose an action stream. All policy work including selection, acquisition, and interrupted jobs is charged using the existing logical-work meter. Keep feasible root incumbent and exact bound invariant. A fixed option is at most four jobs with a hard quantum; then spend remaining comparison horizon on route options. Compare complete policies under identical root work caps.

## Training: paired interventions
32 training roots. At two route-anchored prefix states per root (before any option, and after one route option), replay identical deterministic prefixes in independent cold engine instances. Prefix quantum 4096; paired continuation horizon 8192, intervention quantum 4096. For each of five streams, execute one intervention then route until remaining horizon is exhausted or certificate closed. The route arm is the counterfactual comparator. Count all prefix and continuation work; exact comparison horizon is measured after prefix. Verify identical pre-intervention graph, state/cache contents and work via deterministic canonical fingerprint. This is a root-capped grouped training design (two paired decision sets, ten outcomes per root, no more than two distinct states); outcomes grouped by root, not ten independent samples. Pure bound immediate lower gain must be zero. Record immediate and delayed utility, gap reduction, work and censored options. Fit deterministic ridge heads on signed delayed utility/gap differences versus route. Use shared public root features plus option ID, with option features computed before intervention. No evaluator-only data or evaluation roots in fit. Constant per-stream residual and permuted-label control fit on the same rows. Record fit diagnostics.

## Evaluation
At option boundaries, fixed quantum min(4096, remaining work). Conservative learned route anchor switches only if the estimated signed advantage exceeds 0.25 utility (or 0.25 gap for the certification variant), with route default and no repeated exhausted pure-bound option without graph progress. Controls: route, route-first, pure-bound-first then route, information, optimistic, uniform three-seed random portfolio, constant per-stream residual, permuted-label residual, learned utility residual, learned gap residual. Same quantum, candidate availability and charged selection work for learned/constant/permuted heads; deterministic control uses its normal cheap selection. Eight fresh calibration roots: select smallest of 8192/16384/32768/65536/131072 work units with median route >=8 completed action jobs and >=6 roots completing at least two noncensored options. Evaluate primary and half-primary on 24 ordinary and 24 OOD roots; freeze before evaluation generation. Report paired descriptive intervals versus route and strongest relevant controls, exact bounds/replays, pure bound credit, switches, budget/trace work. Do not promote unless corresponding objective improves on strong controls in both splits. Explicitly distinguish conditional counterfactual prediction from learned recurrence or native BPD benefit.

No external theorem proof, private holdout, new hosted CI or native policy wall-time/RSS claim without actual measurements. Preserve old records and identify any unverifiable earlier timing provenance.
