# MTR-02C: nonlinear obligations, analytic residual and cache locality shift

Registered public development protocol. Successor to MTR-02B. Prior receipts remain immutable.

## Finite decision problem
- 32 latent Boolean assignments with positive integer weights. Public probe definitions induce nonlinear Boolean observations and gated representation choices. Two representations are expensive to prepare; a nonlinear selector determines which representation yields informative witness coordinates. Wrong-branch probes give redundant or misleading partial evidence. Publish a complete assignment, a branch-specific two-coordinate certificate, a coarse Boolean consequence, or abstain. Rewards +24/-36, +12/-18 and +10/-15 respectively, less query/setup costs. Verify each candidate against its Boolean predicate; no source-paper theorem is a label.
- Train on two quadratic construction families. Ordinary uses fresh root generation namespaces; OOD uses cubic selector, changed costs and distractors jointly. Public deterministic roots (development only). Budget 6–8, one-use actions, setup prerequisites, legal informative probes. No nested strategic endorsement chain in this controlled experiment. Exact conditional weighted Bellman solver and explicit per-world replay; a second check uses enumeration of nonadaptive probe subsets and verifies the lower bound on each reachable first-action value evaluated by the learned policy.
- Strong analytic comparator: exhaust affordable prerequisite-closed nonadaptive action subsets, partition current hypotheses by their joint observations, optimize terminal certificate separately in each partition, and subtract subset cost. Replan after each realized observation. For each legal first action, compute a nonadaptive lower bound on exact adaptive Q. Allow redundant suffix probes in the plan accounting; the actual policy may skip them, so the lower bound remains valid. Compare exact, analytic replan, myopic and information-greedy. Record oracle/analytic headroom; no claim that this comparator scales to large games.
- All learned arms use the same canonical current-state token (hypothesis mask, action mask, public prior/probe definitions and budget) plus public action features and analytic Q. Shared exact public terminal macro. Fit Q residual over analytic Q for full-state stateless, recurrent, fixed-damping and pooled-history controls; also fit a direct-Q stateless control. At equal width and training schedule, equal trainable parameter count and three initialization seeds. History models see the same current state plus compressed history; no hidden truth or oracle lookahead enters inputs. Clamp residual to nonnegative at inference because analytic Q is a lower bound; terminal score is always the exact immediate value. Fit on at most four presentations per root, sampled along teacher and exploratory paths. Report root-paired utility, false publication, actual queries/credits, seed sensitivity, same-state/different-history action disagreement if evaluated, and analytic oracle headroom. No promotion unless RLT residual improves on analytic replan and all learned residual controls on both development splits with <=5% extra queries and <=0.005 higher false-publication probability; public intervals descriptive.

## Separate cache experiment
For a subset of fresh evaluation roots, sample exact continuation states and a reproducible repeated-query trace with a stable-locality phase and a disjoint-hot-set shift phase. A fresh exact recurrence computes every distinct requested value and miss work; charge those measured state expansions on misses. Compare no cache, LRU, frequency, work-only, and frequency-times-work admission. Use fixed-width typed-array entries, byte-accounted metadata and an in-budget decaying frequency sketch for frequency and frequency-times-work; report the effective entry capacity for each policy. Equal total bytes (1/4/16 KiB). Cache key includes exact sufficient state and is cleared between roots. All answers compared to exact oracle; measure misses, charged DP expansions, key comparisons, metadata operations and phase metrics. Costs exclude evaluator-side trace/ground-truth storage and are not wall-clock or RSS measurements. No claims of real speedup or native BPD integration.

## Decision
Preserve failures. Report protocol deviations explicitly. Use public development results only. Python integration, independent gradient check, hosted CI, native BPD/TRM/LDT mesh, private holdout and 1B training are out of scope for this pilot.
