# MTR-02G: interruptible constructive portfolios and admissible information relaxation

Registered public development experiment. Preserve MTR-02D/E/F. Goal: measure the value of partial constructive progress, cost-aware strategy selection and tighter proof bounds under hard logical-work caps.

## Worlds and verification
128 latent Boolean witnesses with positive integer priors, nonlinear 4-bit branch and 3-bit payload encoding, prefix-gated single/pair selectors, branch-specific full/partial payload probes, global sketches and cross-cut gates. Train on quadratic tree and coupled-payload families. Ordinary roots are fresh; OOD uses a cubic selector, different cross-cut probe mechanism and shifted costs. Full, partial, branch and coarse certificates and abstention. Exact integer Bellman oracle with independent terminal aggregation, full-scan legality, and weighted per-witness replay. Policy prefix index and cross-cut list reproduce full-scan legal actions and child masks. No nested strategic hypergames or external theorem proof claims.

## Interruptible engine and portfolio
A reached state has exact terminal certificate, optimistic bound and optional expanded actions. A constructive stream uses a fixed local proposal rule (route-oriented, information/credit or immediate terminal payoff) and follows already expanded chosen actions. Its next job expands a single preferred action at a reached state, commits its children with terminal fallbacks, and propagates feasible lower bounds and optimistic upper bounds transactionally through the shared DAG. Preserve the best feasible root plan if a job is interrupted. It is legal for a partial plan to stop at any terminal child. Compare monolithic route and information rollouts, interruptible route and information, a deterministic constructive-first portfolio (route then information then immediate then optimistic), round robin, mass-gap and three seeded random controls, and a learned portfolio. All portfolio arms share the same job definitions; compare complete per-arm logical work with independent cold caches. Charge indexed enumeration, index construction, candidate selection, state aggregation, plan work, feature/scoring, graph commits, backups and aborted work. Replays and root generation/oracle are evaluator-only. Count terminal fallback initialization. No actual CPU/RSS claim from counts.

Admissible information relaxation: give the agent, for free, every outcome of every probe affordable with its remaining credits whose gate intersects its current compatible set; this deliberately ignores future gate prerequisites. The resulting joint partition refines every attainable future terminal partition. Sum best terminal reward over its cells, subtract the minimum cost of a legal first probe for any nonempty research plan, and maximize with immediate terminal reward. This is an upper bound on continuation value. A separate charged bound-refinement job may apply it to a reached state. It must never decrease the lower bound or increase the upper bound; independently audit on visited oracle states for a subset of roots. Report its actual tightening and cost; no promotion merely for smaller uncertified gap if it degrades useful policy.

## Training and evaluation
32 train roots and 8 resource-calibration roots, 24 ordinary and 24 OOD. Fresh deterministic namespaces and content signatures. At most four sampled job presentations per training root. Sample mixed strategy jobs on training trajectories; record initial root bounds, actual gain/gap reduction, charged work, and censoring when a preassigned quantum interrupts a job. Cost model: deterministic regression on log work with one-sided loss for censored lower-bound observations; value/gap model uses observed committed gain (zero if interrupted) with quantum as a public feature. Train only on training roots; freeze coefficients before calibration/evaluation. Learned portfolio prioritizes predicted nonnegative gain divided by predicted cost; a certification variant uses predicted gap reduction. Include a budget-conditioned deterministic portfolio that switches to smaller jobs when remaining work is low. Report job censoring, completion, utility, certified gap, zero-gap and oracle-equality counts, false publication, logical work and state/cache occupancy. Three random seeds averaged within root. Descriptive paired intervals, no private test.

Calibrate primary from 8192/16384/32768/65536/131072/262144 units: smallest budget at which interruptible route completes at least 8 action jobs on median calibration root and at least 6/8 complete one; otherwise largest descriptively with calibration failure. Evaluate at primary and half primary, frozen before generating eval roots. Keep failures. No learned promotion unless corresponding objective beats strongest deterministic controls on both evaluation splits. No native recurrence/BPD/MMS claim, timing/RSS/full CI/private holdout remain separate.
