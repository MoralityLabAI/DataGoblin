# MTR-02F: indexed lazy research search

Registered development experiment, successor to MTR-02E. Preserve MTR-02E source and failed primary budget. Use fresh public generation namespaces.

## Search contract
128 latent witnesses, nonlinear branch/payload encoding, prefix-gated single/pair selectors, branch-specific payload queries and global probes. Positive integer priors, full/partial/branch/coarse certificates and abstention. Train/calibration/ordinary/OOD namespaces disjoint; combined cubic/cost shift is OOD. Deterministic informative observations strictly shrink the compatible mask. Exact state is (mask, research credits). An independently coded integer Bellman recurrence and explicit per-witness replay audit each root.

The policy builds a prefix index from public action gates and charges its construction, storage proxy (number of logical entries), traversal, emitted candidates and partition tests. Indexed candidates must reproduce the original full-scan legal lists including order and outcome masks. Compare eager and lazy node initialization in the same bound engine. Eager leaves compute terminal, every legal one-probe-and-stop plan and two constructive rollouts before committing. Lazy leaves start at the terminal certificate; paid refinement jobs add one-step actions or a constructive rollout. Every feasible incumbent survives a budget stop. Charge primitive scans/visits, cold cache computations, priority features, graph insertion and backups, including interrupted transactions; root public data generation and evaluator replay/oracle are excluded. Count policy materialization if used, or replay the committed graph without materializing it. No claim of byte-bounded process memory: record logical cache/graph occupancy and expose a Node timing/RSS wrapper for a separate native run.

An action job introduces all observation children, starting with their feasible lower and optimistic upper bounds. Unexpanded action upper bound is (maximum full witness reward - cost)*mass. A constructive job adds a verified feasible plan but cannot lower the upper bound by itself. Expanded actions combine child bounds using exact integer mass. Bound propagation is transactional and monotone. Optimistic frontier follows the largest action upper bound, includes its unresolved action job or recursively its unresolved children, and allows available constructive jobs at visited open nodes. Shared work limit is a hard cap. Root bounds contain exact oracle; replay of committed feasible lower plan equals root lower bound.

## Calibration and comparisons
Train 32 roots, calibrate 8 fresh roots (train-only resource selection, no evaluation roots), evaluate 24 ordinary and 24 OOD roots. Use only training states for two deterministic ridge heads, discovery and certification: actual marginal root lower gain or certificate-gap reduction per charged job work, at most four sampled job presentations per root. Record constructive/expansion job type and public features. Fixed controls: mass gap, work-aware gap, random (three seeds), eager mass gap, lazy mass gap with action jobs only, analytic capped contingent planner. All lazy arms with constructive jobs share identical candidate jobs and bounds. Eager comparison is a separate initialization strategy (disclose differences). Analytic constructs a feasible shared base and up to two contingent refinements; all work charged and no evaluator cache. Include fresh cold cache per arm. Use calibration only: select primary budget as smallest power of two >=32768 with median lazy mass-gap action expansions >=8 and at least 6/8 roots completing >=1 action expansion, among 32768/65536/131072/262144/524288. If none qualifies, mark resource calibration failure and use largest descriptively. Evaluate at primary and half primary, fixed before any evaluation root generation. Report root paired descriptive intervals, lower/upper gap, utility, false publication, job counts and charged work. No promotion without improvement over strong deterministic and analytic controls in both cohorts; certification and discovery separate. No model recurrence or native BPD claim. Preserve failures, identify deviations. Timing/RSS wrapper execution, independent private holdout, full CI/native integration remain separate unless explicitly completed.
