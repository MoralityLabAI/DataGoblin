## Recurrent decisions for distillation

The first-verified policy replayed the exact prefix of the fixed-depth policy on all 504 matched episodes. It solved 164/504 versus 141/504, with 2,240 checker calls versus 3,024. Work per verified solution fell from 21.45 to 13.66 checker calls. Of the 164 early successes, 147 first passed at visit 1. The full terminal matrix contained 529 episodes with a regression after an earlier valid answer, including 512 that finished invalid. A compact-model stopping head can use first-valid, pre-verdict and repair targets while the independent checker retains commit authority.

At depth 8 on OOD states, direct answer RMS was 0.614, 0.613 and 0.619 at visits 1, 4 and 8. RLT measured 0.077, 0.306 and 0.618. Damping measured 0.151, 0.413 and 0.618. The schedules changed early state magnitude and converged at the terminal replacement. Carry the measured visit budget and verification work into the next comparison.

The 10% relative random pulse changed the candidate on 60, 32 and 3 of the 126 matched episodes at visits 2, 3 and 4. The untreated first visit matched exactly. The pulse arm gained three net final solves at equal checker cost. These are black-box schedule and perturbation effects in a frozen reservoir.
