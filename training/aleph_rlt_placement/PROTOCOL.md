## Decision

Use mean byte NLL across paired windows as primary and report each corpus. Report correct Lean goals in four checks, both rule turns correct, repaired values and valid counterexamples. Establish neural competence lane by lane. Use train-fit byte unigram, fixed lexical proof order and candidate-pool ceiling, exact traversal, constant repair/counterexample and public cue controls. A failed lane supplies no evidence for a neural mechanism. Treat retained seeds, repeated views and paired edits on one lineage as dependent. The native panel covers up to 48 roots per lane (per corpus in text), the diagnostic panel up to six. These counts are deliberately development-sized.

For promotion to a 12–30M **development** fit, require direct to beat the text unigram and a fixed constant on at least one verified task on at least two seeds. Require a candidate to improve NLL by 0.02 nats/byte or verified-task macro success by 3 percentage points versus direct on at least two seeds, within a single training depth. Hold candidates with mean text NLL more than 1% worse or mean checked-task macro more than 2 points worse. Rank eligible candidates by mean NLL and use checked-task macro to break ties within 0.02 nats. Promote at most the first candidate, direct and its strongest matched damping comparator. If none qualifies, retain the screen and hold paid promotion. A singleton-site coefficient is constant damping by construction. Whole-pass or core-edge improvement at two passes can support placement only. The three-pass matched damping, crossover and equal-budget results govern any coefficient-specific recommendation.

For a full-scale selection use a **new**, author-independent generator/document cohort, frozen after model and analysis choices. Estimate unit counts from paired development variation and the minimum worthwhile effect. Pre-register one primary family of comparisons and simultaneous intervals or Holm tests. Bootstrap documents for text, goal families for Lean, graph lineages for revision, generator families for novel tasks; pair training seeds across arms. Text degradation above 1%, checked aggregate degradation above 2 percentage points or measured throughput loss above 10% holds promotion. Require sufficient resolution on critical tasks, and a quality improvement against the strongest prespecified control. A development ranking is not a fresh confirmation. The strict-1B direct run remains the reference recommendation when placement is unresolved.

**Cross-depth tie rule recorded before the first mixed native score.** Within each depth apply the above ranking. If both depths produce an eligible finalist, compare their relative text NLL change versus their respective same-depth direct arms. Choose the larger reduction. Within 0.5 percentage point choose two passes for lower compute. Replicate at width 256 with the same training budget, three seeds, and the strongest distinct coefficient control. Where the matched scalar is mathematically identical, report the identity and omit the duplicate large fit. The finite-difference and reversed-clock analyses are post-fit diagnostics.
