# ALEPH-01 and 02 registration

The independent revision probe at `d68c0c7` already completed 21 fits on old-answer edits. This experiment examines internal corruption and verified repair tables or counterexample witnesses on previously unseen Boolean rule compositions. The dense proxy uses DataGoblin's causal GQA/RoPE/SwiGLU block for the prelude, two shared middle blocks U and V, and coda. Four positions are four candidate assignments. An external bitwise truth-table verifier grades predictions. Its intermediate answers and hidden seed never enter the network.

Fix width 32, seeds 137, 163 and 191, batch 32, 192 updates, AdamW .002, gradient cap 1 and identical four-position examples, perturbation distributions and initial backbone weights within each seed. Each stream includes two table-repair families (corrupted premises of a proof step and application of the wrong rule), and two witness families (inconsistent printed steps and a misleading proposed solution). An exact repair must fix all four rows. A counterexample must identify a row that falsifies the printed transition or solution. Select a single row by maximum logit. A fixed verifier supplies all supervision. Copy the printed conclusion for a repair or choose assignment zero for a witness as the cheap baseline.

At two visits compare direct, pass damping (also exactly the two-pass RLT clock), fixed .25 source reread, fixed .25 local reread, learned source gate and identically parameterized local gate. Reread once between U and V on the second visit. Local means the second visit's actual, already-perturbed input. Learned source and local gates start at .25 and use the same two scalar parameters conditioned on relative anchor displacement. At three visits compare direct, damping (5/12,5/12,1), whole-pass RLT (1/3,1/2,1), a two-scalar magnitude-conditioned clock and a parameter-matched two-scalar visit-conditioned clock. All learned clocks start at constant damping and keep the final full update. Clock and gate parameters are separate from the shared backbone.

Intervene after the first complete visit. A targeted direction is the model's prelude difference from replacing two premise columns with the public, potentially faulty proof claims and negating a third premise. Independent isotropic noise is the matched control. Normalize each direction to the same per-example RMS relative to the pass endpoint, with prespecified strengths 0, .5 and 1.25. The targeted direction uses public printed evidence without verifier labels. Train on all three strengths and both directions. The four absent ordered compositions are OR-after-XOR, XOR-after-OR, implication-after-AND and AND-after-implication. Freeze checkpoint and source hashes before drawing new private cases within these families. Evaluate 16 fresh examples per task and held composition, paired across strengths, directions and arms. Score clean cases once. Family-level aggregates weight each composition and task equally. The same held cases also support descriptive train/inference clock crossovers.

Gate inputs and perturbation normalization use hidden features at the same token. This preserves suffix causality and also matches the relative RMS across the full example. Report final bitwise NLL, all-or-nothing repair accuracy, valid witness rate, per-pass verified success, activation RMS, proposed and realized relative displacement, successive update cosine, directional Jacobian gain and central second-difference curvature. The latter two are sampled along matched random directions and do not estimate a spectral norm. Report measured CPU wall time, traced supported eager-operation FLOPs, unique parameters, logical block visits and processed positions. The short fixed-table proxy does not measure natural-language perplexity.

**Promotion gates.** First require direct to exceed its task-matched cheap baseline by at least five verified percentage points in each of the repair and witness groups on mean held scores. Fixed source must exceed fixed local and direct by at least two points on pooled high-strength accuracy, beat both on every seed, and lose at most two clean points to direct. Learned source must clear the same margins against learned local and fixed source. Three-pass RLT must beat damping on every seed at high strength, gain at least two points there on average and show a high-minus-clean advantage at least one point larger. A magnitude clock must pass the same tests against damping and outperform the visit-conditioned clock at high strength on every seed. A candidate also needs better high-strength success than its strongest matched rival in at least three of four unseen compositions, and positive high-strength gain against that rival for both targeted and random perturbations. Failing these gates retains the direct and damping controls. These tests justify at most another local scale, never a paid run.

The fit and score commands are `bounded.py --wall 1800 --core 0 architecture_lab.py train --out OUT` and `bounded.py --wall 600 --core 0 architecture_lab.py score --out OUT`. Higher-scale continuations can supply `--width`, `--steps`, `--batch`, `--seeds` and `--arms` in a new output directory. All checkpoints in the requested design must exist and pass source and file checks before the scorer creates a private seed.
