## Held tasks

The repair input uses native register IDs 0–3, SET 16, ADD 17, REPAIR 260 and SEP 262. A prompt shows a proposed register, the initial register and a sequence of edits. Its required output is the correct register after every edit. The proposal may already be correct. Public training uses one to three instructions with at most one reset. Its nine operator words are S, A, AA, SA, AS, AAA, SAA, ASA and AAS. The held grammar independently fixes four families with three distinct lengths each.

| Program family | Length four | Length five | Length six |
|---|---|---|---|
| No reset | AAAA | AAAAA | AAAAAA |
| Initial reset | SAAA | SAAAA | SAAAAA |
| Interior reset | ASAA | AASAA | AAASAA |
| Multiple resets | SSAA | SASAA | ASASAA |

Draw eight distinct counterfactual orbits per word. An orbit exposes all four choices of the last reset operand or, in its absence, the initial register. All other operands, the rival proposal and suffix stay fixed. Each orbit contains all four correct outputs and three genuinely wrong proposals. Thus there are 96 program clusters and 384 observed cases. Score exact corrected register as the primary measure, and all-four-orbit accuracy and incorrect-proposal-only accuracy as diagnostics. Weight families equally, then lengths, orbits and the four variants. Check every answer by forward execution and independent four-input truth-table composition.

The earlier native repair stream displayed longer traces while supervising a single marked transition. Reject any held orbit if any constituent program occurs as a length-four to length-six contiguous computation in any of those 512 training or 96 development traces. Disallow duplicate computations across the new orbits, including duplicates whose untrusted proposal differs.

## Controls and competence

Evaluate prespecified public-only cues. Repair controls return zero, the rival proposal, the initial state, the last operand, the last reset operand (initial state if none), the mode of the visible proposal/start/operands, the last two operands added modulo four, or the result of applying the final public operation to either the initial state or the preceding operand. A stronger bounded repair starts at the last reset (or initial state) and executes only the last two surviving additions. Graph controls return zero, the starting node, the first unpatched hop, the first currently visible hop, a two-hop truncated walk, or one original hop on the first turn and the disclosed patch destination on the second. Include two stronger public controls. A stale predictor follows the full original graph and ignores the patch. A bounded patch repair also follows the original graph, then corrects a changed edge if at most one further hop remains. An exact native walk and the exact register executor are verification ceilings. The strongest cue is the maximum over all eligible controls on that entire lane. In paired resampling take that maximum anew in each draw, retaining cue-selection uncertainty.

For each arm require independently measured held text loss below its training-byte unigram, and verified performance over the strongest shallow cue on at least one of the two tasks. Each qualifying comparison must clear its direction on at least two of three matched training seeds. On the winning checked task its paired 97.5% one-sided lower bound for the gain must exceed zero. The two prespecified checked-task alternatives share the error budget. Report each model's complete verifier ceilings and every cue score even if the model is below chance. Report results for both A and B.

## Paired contrasts

Use 20,000 deterministic paired bootstrap draws with public statistics seed 202611253. The primary bounds condition on the three matched training seeds, averaging each seed equally. Resample four author-documents within each of the three registered text strata and compute equal-document NLL. For reasoning resample four repair families with replacement and two native graph regimes with replacement, separately. Within selected repair families keep all three lengths equally weighted and resample eight complete orbits per length. Within a selected native regime resample 32 complete lineages. Keep both patch variants, both turns and every orbit member together. Report a sensitivity that also resamples the three matched seeds jointly across A, B and tasks, and a fixed-family sensitivity that resamples only within each of the named families. These bootstrap bounds describe this finite experiment with few top-level families. Append raw paired seed differences and leave-one-family-out gains.

Define text gain as (A NLL − B NLL) / A NLL, and checked gain as B checked macro − A checked macro. B needs competent anchors, a text-gain one-sided 97.5% lower bound of at least −1%, a checked-gain lower bound of at least −2 percentage points and comparable training throughput of at least 90% of A. Then either (i) checked gain must be at least +2 points with a strictly positive lower bound and at least two positive seed gains, or (ii) text gain must be at least +1% relative with a strictly positive lower bound and at least two positive seed gains. A single best checkpoint or a control passing its own competence gate cannot satisfy this promotion rule. Record exact seed averages, uncertainty, route outcomes and failed criteria. The operator's separate paired CPU benchmark supplies the throughput check.
