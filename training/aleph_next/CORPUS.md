# Turning small researchers into a training gym

The Gym Metadata collator indexes the immutable protocols and participant checkpoint hashes. Model Atlas records carry architecture, recurrence, corpus recipe, aggregate phenotype, mechanism, parent and known failure. The discovery index has its original custody setting. The independent corpus exporter reads the **training manifests and frozen participant weights** and generates fresh mathematical exercises from the public training grammar. Its two entry points take no evaluation files. Both exports use existing DataGoblin interfaces. [Model Atlas](../../src/datagoblin/model_atlas.py) · [Gym Metadata](../../src/datagoblin/gym_metadata.py) · [Aleph packed stream](../aleph_rlt_placement/packing.py)

**Posterior repair.** C, D and MLP senders each emit one hard bit before the future query. A frozen heterogeneous receiver forecasts a binary outcome using only public query, side observation and message. The verifier enumerates eight equiprobable worlds. After commitment, the audit student gets the public code table, query masks, observed side/receipt and receiver forecast. Its job is to correct the forecast as a reduced fraction. Of 716 distinct prompts, 410 initial forecasts missed the answer by more than ten percentage points. This is a post-decision audit task; the deployed receiver never gets the verifier's code table.

**Costed receiver choice.** Each slate has three actual fitted readers. For each, the environment computes the conditional binary Brier loss with its separately fitted side-only and valid-bit controls. The prompt supplies these public calibration losses quantized to millionths and an explicit charge for the upload. Select the reader and channel minimizing scored loss plus charge. The bit price moves across the best side/valid indifference point; negative examples teach when to decline a message. Of 648 checked slates, 488 provisional choices had strictly positive regret. The numbers are supplied for this arithmetic and option-selection gym. Inferring those numbers on an unseen family remains a separate transfer problem.

The combined export contains 1,364 train-only exercises from six native LDT families, nine frozen senders and actual neural, logistic, empirical and structured readers. It has 490,496 nonpadding pilot-byte positions and 10,369 scored positions after packing. Five response positions fall on segment/context boundaries without a preceding supervised pair. The byte tokenizer is a 257-symbol pilot; the strict model requires retokenization under its frozen 49,152-entry tokenizer and new shard hashes. The bundle includes a train-only human-readable receipt per row and an immutable SQLite registry. None of the private evaluation draws, per-world held labels or ranking results enter this stream. Each corpus row's supervised span starts at the answer. Its verifier receipt hash is checked by the registry. [Exporter](research_stream.py) · [Costed options](research_options.py)

An external researcher can now contribute a candidate policy or a decoder specialization and be assessed on a common question. For a binary outcome the best squared-loss forecast is the conditional mean. A sender's ideal value equals the expected squared change from the side-only posterior to the side-plus-receipt posterior. An operational team's value also depends on its two readers' excess risks. Subtract a per-bit charge and compare with other teams on the same public information set. This gives compactification a falsifiable outcome for each contribution. A new sender can raise ideal value and lower partner utility at the same time, as the C reader demonstrated. A heterogeneous pair that helps on familiar programs can still lose on new mathematical rules.
