## Gates before starting the paid first leg

1. **Supply and representativeness.** Fill each deficit above with newly admitted presentations under the frozen tokenizer and mixture. “General” is currently mathematics textbooks only. “Custom research” is predominantly graph routing. Diversify general prose and research tasks, add independent book/author groups per evaluation role, and verify the lineage, rights, duplicates, difficulty and target counts of every new release. Freeze and dry-walk the full 10B schedule against a new immutable index. Repeating this small corpus would change the recipe.
2. **Target implementation.** Connect the exact five-lane stream and masked next-token loss to a BF16 accelerator backend. Establish SPMD topology, vocabulary sharding, collective loss/gradient normalization, segment isolation, activation rematerialization and memory headroom on the intended hardware. Compare a short deterministic batch against the CPU reference including finite gradients, loss and shifted masks. The PR contains a CPU reference and a geometry check. A runnable qualified TPU entry point remains outstanding.
3. **Durable target restart.** Publish the checkpoint only after every rank's shard and global cursor are hash-verified. Crash before and after publication, restore on the same topology, and compare ordered token IDs, masks, counters, step, LR, logits and optimizer state to uninterrupted execution. Device precision tolerances must be fixed before the numerical test. Verify external storage capacity for recovery plus at least two completed checkpoints.
4. **Throughput, spend and operator approval.** A single 10B run at the operator's estimate of 55 hours and 350 credits needs 50,505 useful positions/s inclusive of evaluation, I/O and recovery (about 6.36 credits/hour). The analytical two-pass cost is approximately 1.053 × 10²⁰ FLOPs. Real topology, billing rate and memory have not been measured here. Measure them on free hardware if available, record the inclusive estimate and request explicit authorization before any credit-consuming work. The first-round ledger records zero accelerator credits.
