# ZKL-17: term-calculus curriculum candidate

Status: native synthetic differential audit passed; the Nova and typed Lean CI gates are separate. Source is original to the research collaboration, retained at [zk-rusto-no-bitcoino](https://github.com/patrickdugan/zk-rusto-no-bitcoino/tree/df6402b769e80d5ea939bd78995317ceeaa936a2/integrations/zkl-nova-calibration). Upstream zkLean remains a read-only research reference with no declared license; no upstream proof corpus or source is admitted.

The grammar uses variables (de Bruijn indices), inert constants, lambda, application. A source-checked certificate has three semantic row kinds: Lift(t,k,u), Sub(t,k,s,u), Eq(t,u). The 17 local rule cases cover shifting, capture-avoiding substitution including open replacements, and the equivalence/congruence closure of beta. A canonical public term arena precedes the proof log. The first circuit profile caps both arenas at 64 nodes/rows. It verifies raw syntax conversion only, without a dependent type-checking relation; it exposes term syntax and has no privacy claim.

Native finite evidence: 4096 generated substitutions compared with an independent depth-accumulator algorithm, 4096 generated beta certificates replayed, five fixtures covering 34 rows and all 17 cases, six coherent mutations rejected. On 768 disjoint generated beta redexes, shared certificates total 1291/1671/1675 rows at component depths 3/5/7, versus 1393/1918/2148 for unfolded certificates. 766/768 shared cases fit the current capacities. This is a memoization baseline, not a proof of minimal certificate size. No model trained or promotion gate met.

Proposed training record (conceptual, not released): source root hash, grammar and arena schema versions, public term arena, current obligations, candidate rule and earlier row references, checked output judgment and native checker receipt. Include valid but redundant rows and independently generated rejected mutations as separate labels; never let a candidate claim its own verification. Train to predict rule/children and reuse existing derivations, with a fixed certificate budget and separate checked solve coverage. Keep all transformed versions, beta steps and mutations of a source term in the same root family. Reserve independently generated sealed evaluator roots before any neural fitting. Compare to deterministic memoization and exact/reference algorithms; no credit for invalid outputs.

Before promoting to a training release: establish the source artifact's license/provenance under the repository policy, independently audit parser and checker, record allowed purpose and custody, bind the exact grammar and cost profile, and use independent environment-owned verification. Do not copy the public pilot seeds into sealed evaluation. Do not treat native row count as measured Nova cost until the circuit is calibrated.
