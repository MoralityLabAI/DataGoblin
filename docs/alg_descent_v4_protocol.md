Charge product verification on success and rejection, and one per cache hit. Public generator structure is allowed; no validity oracle at inference.

Compute unrestricted positive ordered-partition overhead lower bound L(d) for arities 2,3,4,8 by dynamic programming. For a candidate, O=d+k+sum prefix degrees. Its structural score O+sum L(children) is independent of the quadratic invariant. Train a smoothed empirical validity prior using public arity, balanced partition indicator and field characteristic from train roots only. Symbolic controls: masked v3 heuristic, structural bound, structural bound plus retry premium C*(1/p_hat-1). The premium follows from the adjacent exchange rule C/p+V under independent fixed success probabilities and fixed downstream costs; it is a heuristic with correlated candidates and changing posterior. 

Neural arms: separate valid-action downstream structural excess (nonnegative over L, normalized by d) and validity-logit residual over empirical prior. Features are relative-degree/shape features and candidate descriptors, no absolute degree in scale-free arm; absolute log degree in scale-aware arm; GRU on recent rejection features with matched same-checkpoint reset ablation. Three seeds 17/31/53, 12 epochs, same data and optimizer. Score successful overhead plus learned excess and retry premium. Report all arms and descriptive root bootstrap. If the primary comparator is weaker than another symbolic arm, report that limitation and require the same conditions against the strongest observed symbolic comparator for a learned superiority claim.
