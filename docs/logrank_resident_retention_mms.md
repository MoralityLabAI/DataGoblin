# Resident-aware separator retention

Source: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/dcc92fedecadf967626d922fb8a9d169841aa71c/experiments/logrank_bpd_22.

22 replaces the warm-cache proxy with exact pinned-target lazy-fill work, accounting for current valid residents, repeated demand and actual fill counts. The retention arm additionally forecasts next-epoch demand from the current public batch and samples future invalidation owners independently of realized updates. Local update site is public; uniform forecast uses four sampled scenarios. The factorial design separates source popularity and update locality. No future query outcomes, native training or confirmation.

18 worlds, 24 admitted structured conditions and 12 dense scope refusals; 1,056 trajectories, 67,584 exact arm-query comparisons, 385,937 cache transitions, and 1,536 independent current work/fill matches. At 96D hubs/64KiB, retention saves 2.2-8.7% counted arithmetic vs planned cold across four regimes. At 16KiB resident beats warm marginal in each aggregate, with little additional benefit from retention. The original exhaustive candidate planner is much slower; a sparse exact delta implementation matches 96 exposed-root trajectories and reduces 64KiB retention runtime by 2.90-3.35x, still slower than planned cold. The cap is logical retained bytes, not total workspace or RSS. Main 96D timing overlaps part of the 12D audit; isolated diagnostic alternates implementation order.

MMS recommendation: preserve planned cold at roomy budgets and warm marginal at tight budgets as strong baselines. Use exact lazy-fill cost for bounded local refinement of an existing plan; gate planning by actual elapsed overhead, estimated additional savings and validity lifetime. Test additions/swaps on a small public shortlist, with independently sampled forecast scenarios and a cheap no-change baseline. Keep exact reference, invalidation/version checks and dense refusals. Native integration and confirmation remain outstanding.
