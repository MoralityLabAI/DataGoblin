# Adapter-receipt LDT sorter v16

V16 is a frozen corrective sorter experiment. No official development or
heldout result is recorded yet. It responds to the valid negative v2 adapter
tournament without reopening or fitting to its spent heldout labels.

## Development-only diagnosis

The retained v15 shadow model saw 12 public development receipt arrays. Across
their 48 candidate statuses it correctly marked all 12 exact-key controls
`DOMINATED` and all 8 true `SELECTED` candidates, but it also mapped 16 other
`DOMINATED` and all 12 `UNRESOLVED` trainable candidates to `SELECTED`. It
therefore recovered zero exact status vectors and zero exact selected sets.

Only that aggregate development confusion and the public development receipt
shape inform v16. V2 heldout row labels, targets, error identities, and
per-array outcomes are forbidden design inputs.

## Corrective comparison

Every learned arm uses the same fresh data, seeds, optimizer, exact normalized
status-vector likelihood, hidden width, loop count, and hard-cap contract:

- an independent-candidate exact-global control;
- the v15 edge-difference encoder retrained on the representative receipt data;
- a cross-bound encoder with a factorized decoder; and
- the matched cross-bound global primary.

The new encoder exposes raw candidate-lower minus neighbor-upper and reverse
interval differences for the four material metrics, plus raw parameter-bucket
differences. It also applies a fixed eight-dimensional random projection to
each directed edge before mean/min/max neighbor pooling. Its 74 features are
permutation-equivariant and use no metric tolerance, target, rationale,
architecture identity, verifier result, or heldout value. The projection seed
is frozen at 16031 and is not trainable.

A fixed membrane routes unequal `safety_qualified` values before learned pair
or status quality. Its result is disclosed in every model receipt and is never
credited to the learned architecture. The unchanged v15 checkpoint and exact
interval policy remain diagnostic-only controls and cannot be selected.

## Fresh evidence surface

Development and heldout each contain six disjoint mixed-mechanism lineages,
36 arrays, 888 orientation-balanced ordered pairs, and 196 content-free
tournament reports. Each lineage includes:

- clear conservative dominance;
- three-seed interval overlap with genuine `UNRESOLVED` statuses;
- a mixed Pareto frontier;
- parameter-equivalent candidates;
- a cross-neighbor frontier that tests false “Frankenstein” dominance; and
- one safety-sentinel pressure for the fixed membrane.

Sizes four, five, and six are all supported. The disposable generation smoke
confirmed every preference, rationale, and array status in all six development
lineages, while heldout row and class counts remain undisclosed. The smoke is
discovery-only and cannot be merged into an official run.

## Frozen gates

Seed selection is gate-first over seeds 311, 367, and 421. The cross-bound
global primary must clear the existing 1.0 safety and swap gates, 0.75 pair
macro-F1 and abstain gates, 0.80 status macro-F1, 0.65 exact status-vector,
0.75 exact selected-set, 0.50 worst-fold exact gates, and 0.20 maximum ECE.
It must additionally improve both mean exact metrics by at least 0.05 over the
same-data v15 encoder and be no worse on either worst-fold exact metric.

Only one qualified cross-bound global seed may enter the confirmation packet.
The single heldout join repeats the overall exact gates and separately applies
them to the five all-safe primary-domain pressures. A pass authorizes only a
newly frozen downstream shadow preregistration.

## Official sequence

After committing the frozen harness, generate a new external fixture, run all
12 candidate/seed shards under the WSL cgroup wrapper, merge the complete
matrix, apply gate-first and comparative selection, and freeze the one-primary
packet. The independent evaluator may open the sealed labels once only after
the no-label bindings pass. The separately frozen auditor must then reproduce
the selection, resource and custody checks, exact metrics, and operational
conclusion.

No generated fixture, labels, predictions, model state, checkpoint, or run
directory belongs in the repository.
