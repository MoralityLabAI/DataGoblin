# ALG-DESCENT-11 / Horn proof planning prospective CPU development protocol

2026-11-27. Separate synthetic proof-planning task, no polynomial factoring or discrete-log inference. A root is a public acyclic weighted propositional Horn rule graph with three initial facts and one goal. Every rule has immutable ID, head, premise set and positive integer proof cost. The solution witness is not supplied to the planner; it must discover a forward proof by exact rule applications. The full rule graph and current proved set are public (so exact planning is possible). Goal closure requires the recorded derivation to replay from initial facts; rule IDs are bound to the immutable registry, not proposer-supplied premises. No hidden witness, private solver features or labels at evaluation.

Use deterministic random generator: ordered heads 3..n-1, 2-3 alternative rules per head, 1-3 distinct earlier premises and costs 1..7; train/IID uniform and clustered prerequisite families, OOD shared-bottleneck family and larger uniform/clustered graphs. Every rule head reachable by induction. Train 48 roots seeds 610000..610047, n=10..13 cyclic, alternating uniform/clustered; IID 16 roots seeds 620000..620015 n=10..13; OOD 24 roots seeds 630000..630023 n=16..18 cyclic, alternating shared/uniform/clustered. Use train-only exact monotone-state Bellman labels (minimum additive proof cost, terminal 0) on up to 96 sampled reachable states per root; no teacher query during evaluation search. Compute offline exact costs on all eval roots as privileged reference. Public heuristic features only: normalized known and relevant facts, ready rules, relaxed hmax/hadd/FF union cost, goal-rule premise coverage, and graph/ready summaries. Model learns residual remaining cost over FF; compare small MLP and weight-tied three-update model (report counts), three seeds 19/41/67, fixed training schedule, no OOD tuning.

Same best-first search engine with exact proved-set key and best-g duplicate handling, full public rule scan per expanded state, exact applicability, predecessor proof; stop at first popped goal or 64 nonterminal expansions; charge rule inspections, queue/state counts, and replay cost separately from returned proof weight. Arms: uniform-cost, admissible hmax A*, additive relaxation, FF union heuristic, weighted FF (weight 2), learned MLP/three-update residual (g+predicted remaining cost). Offline exact Bellman reference not a budget-matched competitor. All policies share root/initial facts and caps. Record closure, mean proof cost / offline optimum on jointly closed roots, expansions and inspected rules, inference/wall time, state duplicates; report failed routes separately, no treating incomplete proof as cheap solved proof. Root-level paired results aggregate seeds; gate for learned planning: match or improve closure vs strongest symbolic control (lexicographic closure then mean normalized proof cost), improve mean normalized proof cost by >=5% on jointly closed OOD roots OR match proof cost within 1% and reduce mean expansions >=20%, with >=15/24 root wins on the corresponding metric, no wall-time claim unless measured. Distinguish budgeted satisficing and optimality. Independent verifier reconstructs all proofs with sets and immutable rules and sums costs; audit Bellman recurrence on all train/eval states, no hidden solution at inference. No native BPD/MMS integration, cryptographic claim, Lean formalization, or sealed test; report actual deviations and failures.
