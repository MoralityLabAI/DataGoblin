# Public basis induction and confidence-gated verifier allocation

Source: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/be5ff2f9a173a6d8a716c9ddf2b1cbf11e9ad75e/experiments/logrank_bpd_13.

13 evaluates unequal costs, end-to-end BP and a ridge imitation controller on fresh noisy parity worlds. At small widths 9/11 and noise .15, public induced-basis BP scores .01885 weighted Brier with mean extra cost 7.25/24, raw BP .23954 at full cost. On identical exact-policy traces the induced basis gives .01428 vs raw .22929. Ridge acquisition does not beat the direct BP heuristic at .15. At width 48 and .15 noise, induced BP's nominal 90%-confidence answers achieve only 65.15% covered-weight accuracy (four worlds); the stopping rule is not deployment-ready. At .35 noise no scale arm issues confident answers.

Implementation recommendation: use a public ordered GF(2) frame of observation masks and cost-ordered candidates before factorization. Keep raw and alternate public bases for cross-checks. Exact span gating prevents claiming information about unobserved directions. Approximate confidence needs independent calibration; frame agreement alone is not proof. Record initial and extra verifier costs separately, score actual approximate predictions, and compare exact posterior on the same trace at small rank. Future work should use independently calibrated frame ensembles, verifier-backed residual repair and bounded exact conditional decoding. Match resource scaling across widths, and retain random and fixed-budget controls. No native schema integration or confirmation release.
