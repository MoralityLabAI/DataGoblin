# MOLT-17 exact cut feasibility

The standard-library verifier is ported from
[LoopedTransformers #415](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/415),
source revision 3b45fef9d97e0ae07381fd3ec68ba68aad2d5dd3. It independently checks rational primal and dual
witnesses on an exported problem bundle rather than using a floating-point
solver classification as authority.

    python -m datagoblin.training_gym.molt_exact_carry_certificate \
      --problems /tmp/molt17/export/problems.json --out /tmp/molt17-exact.json

The exact_obstruction status rules out the declared immediate legal winner
in the specified chart and enlarged physical dose ball, conditional on
the exported constants. A scheduler can stop spending optimization budget
on that particular cut target after separately checking the binding and
source origin. A different chart, dose, cut, observer or checkpoint needs a
new witness. The exact_primal status supplies a strict legal winner in the
real-valued cut model; independently replay and verify the actual task
before use. Unresolved carries no authority.

The receipt verifies neither the origin of the problem constants, native
floating-point execution, nor the complete suffix or an LDT refinement.
No training promotion or hard resource claim.
