# PT-01: first capped research post-training campaign

This is a proposed 35-RU experiment envelope. Run first during travel only if the owner explicitly authorizes that particular batch; otherwise retain for after travel. Use the latest verified Aleph checkpoint available at execution, with its parent and tokenizer pinned. The known direct-A 10M engineering pilot is an eligible engineering parent, not a claim of language or research competence.

## Hypothesis

Research-state transitions with independently measured outcomes improve next-experiment choice and cost-adjusted verified research yield over imitation of the same teacher demonstrations alone.

## Zero-RU preflight (PT-00)

- Check current checkpoint availability, exact tokenizer and architecture, restore optimizer/RNG or record intentional reset.
- Reuse existing SFT and preference exporters; map one RLT/Lean task and one EvalMath research decision family into the transition contract. Use a tiny deterministic fixture and CPU-only replay.
- Pin code, root roster, independent evaluator, rewards, train/dev/locked-test split and baseline. Run leak checks across roots, branching descendants, near duplicates and quotient groups.
- Test policy masks, outcome provenance, terminal/truncated semantics, cost reservations, failure recovery, replay determinism, and secrets redaction.
- Verify provider rights, account meter, billing increment, in-flight upper bound, cancellation behavior and persistent artifact destination before any charged launch. If an upper bound cannot be enforced, stop.

Reallocate unused lane budget only within a recorded approved campaign policy and never raise its ceiling. Avoid reserving a GPU while waiting on a teacher if billing continues. Reserve the worst-case incremental liability before any submission; count failed jobs and retries. Stop dispatching when settled spend plus open reservations plus proposed maximum would exceed the cap. On stale/unknown meter, failed cancellation or uncertain liability, fail closed. Persist the ledger before dispatch and reconcile external job IDs after restart; never resubmit blindly.

## Experimental ladder

PT-01A: deterministic action-selection and outcome prediction on small research environments. Compare untrained parent, fixed heuristic, raw demonstration SFT, weighted imitation (matched training targets and recurrent-cell budget). Include a shuffled-outcome control preserving target frequency.

PT-01B: same-state branches, fixed independent verifier, preference or supported-action value learning. Evaluate on disjoint research families, calibration and cost-adjusted returns. Train on executed actions only for value/reward targets; preserve unexecuted proposals as context.

PT-02 (new authorization): fresh DataGoblin student rollouts on constrained action interfaces. Compare reward-guided policy update against matched imitation and compute controls. Keep teacher as a proposal/evaluation-planning agent; reward authority stays with the environment. Expand to free-form multi-step research only after constrained competence and verifier coverage.

PT-03 (later): recurrent stopping and budget policies; compare fixed loops, learned stopping, matched damping/RLT and validated quotient reuse. Charge actual recurrent visits and verifier calls, not just output tokens. Use matched task/compute exposures and retain quality at several inference budgets.

## Evaluation and gate

Primary: independently verified research utility per root at a frozen cost cap, with paired root-level comparisons and uncertainty. Secondary: valid experiment selection, falsification/revision, calibrated predictions, correct stopping, proof/test replay, language/task retention, actual RU/teacher-token/recurrent-visit consumption. Count timeouts and invalid outputs; preserve non-results. Preregister meaningful effect and sample size before seeing dev labels; no numeric efficacy threshold is claimed in PT-00. Pilot is descriptive if underpowered. A candidate only promotes if the predeclared efficacy and retention gates pass, provenance and billing checks pass, and the independent evaluator confirms. Report all arms, failures and exact uncertainty. The sealed test remains closed until a separately frozen confirmation.

## Handoff packet for next 35 RU

Record parent/candidate hashes; immutable data manifest; reward/scorer and split revisions; hypothesis/prediction; measured outcomes and CIs; full cost reconciliation; failed attempts; replay commands; remaining questions; one next falsifiable hypothesis, expected decision value and estimated maximum cost. Explicitly recommend stop, repeat, change method or promote. No next campaign starts without a new authorization. For travel, keep every phase restartable with uploaded evidence and a single concise report; never require the owner to stay online to terminate a capped job.
