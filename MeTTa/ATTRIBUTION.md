Original copyright ownership remains with the respective authors. A repository's license declaration is evidence of its declared terms; individual content that embeds third-party text or derivatives needs an additional rights check. The catalog therefore flags the `SICP_book` directory and oversized `aunt-kg` datasets for manual review. Source snapshots or full extracted forms must retain the appropriate upstream copyright and permission notices when redistributed.

The source-catalog Git object identifiers prove that an intake matches the pinned code tree. They do not attest that executing a MeTTa program is safe, syntactically accepted, or semantically sound. A native Hyperon run with a recorded environment and independent result comparison is needed before adding execution labels.

Out-of-distribution Storyworld research/evaluation files are quarantined from curation training. The general Storyworld examples are reserved for OOD study until split review. Generated synthetic curation tasks carry the `SIMULATED` evidence type and do not stand in for a steward-issued training release.
